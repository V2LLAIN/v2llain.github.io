    About Me

    Hello World! I'm Hyeongchan Im. I'm currently a research intern at KAIST in the KIRC Lab, advised by Prof. Mun-Yong Yi.

    My research centers on Multimodal Learning with a core focus on Vision-Language-(Action) Models, a particular focus on Medical AI to bridge the gap between AI and human communication, and Deepfake Detection for detecting high-fidelity facial manipulation.
